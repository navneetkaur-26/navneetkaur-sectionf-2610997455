Famous Tourist Places

1. Taj Mahal

A white marble mausoleum located in Agra, known as a symbol of love and one of the Seven Wonders of the World.

2. Hawa Mahal

A palace in Jaipur built from red and pink sandstone with unique high screen walls for royal women.

3. Golden Temple

The holiest gurdwara of Sikhism located in Amritsar, known for its gilded central shrine.

4. Gateway of India

An arch monument built during the 20th century in Mumbai, overlooking the Arabian Sea.

5. Qutub Minar

A UNESCO World Heritage Site in Delhi and the tallest brick minaret in the world.
