College Terminology

Lecture
A class session where a professor teaches academic concepts.
Credit
A unit measuring the weight of a course in the academic curriculum.
Syllabus
An outline of the topics covered in a course.
Hostel
Residential facility provided for students inside or near campus.
Midterm
An examination conducted halfway through an academic term.
